Virtual viewers are model-generated perspectives, not a representative survey. Estimates may reflect model assumptions and should never be presented as actual platform analytics.

Data & limitations

Know what is stored and what is inferred

Video files and report records persist locally in the data directory. Actual-performance submissions are kept separate from predictions and are not automatically used for model calibration. Scene anchors are heuristic. There are no validated accuracy claims or statistical confidence intervals.
